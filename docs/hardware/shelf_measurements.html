Shelf tape measurements, M1–M14

The robot can't touch the side panels' tops or the topmost board squarely, so the shelf's own dimensions come from a tape. They are tied into the robot's frame by the two slot marks on the top board that the robot already touched (CALIBRATION.md). Measure every blue item in millimetres, fill the table, and paste the block it produces.

measure with the tape already touched by the robot (don't measure)

Stand where the robot is, facing the shelf's open front. "Left" and "right" are your left and right from there. The left slot is the one on your left.

The slot marks are the two spots on the top board touched for "top shelf, left slot" and "top shelf, right slot" (233 mm apart). M1–M4 are measured from those exact spots. If you can't re-find them, say so before measuring.

The shelf is turned about 19° to the robot. Measure along the shelf itself, not along the room: M1 and M2 parallel to the boards' front edge, M3 and M4 square to it.
